The increasing demand for digital platforms in tourism highlights the need for systems that can effectively promote local experiences while helping travelers discover relevant destinations. However, many Community-Based Tourism (CBT) operators still rely on fragmented promotional channels and lack centralized digital platforms to enhance their visibility. This study introduces LokalKita, a webbased tourism recommendation application designed to support local hosts in marketing their products and experiences while providing travelers with personalized discovery features. The system integrates an AI-powered search function, preference-based recommendation display, and multi-role management modules supporting travelers, hosts, and administrators. The platform enables users to browse, search, filter, like, and save experiences, while hosts can manage content submissions under administrative moderation. Functional validation and usability assessment indicate that the application operates reliably and provides a user-friendly interface for experience exploration. Overall, LokalKita demonstrates practical potential in supporting sustainable local tourism promotion through an integrated digital platform.
